**What Happens During the Bilona Process?**

In the traditional Bilona process, milk is first set into curd. The curd is churned to separate butter, and that butter is heated into ghee. This curd and butter stage is the practical difference to ask about when comparing it with ghee described as being made from separated cream.
Gentle heating drives off moisture and separates milk solids from the finished fat. The aroma and texture that result are part of why many households enjoy this kind of ghee. A grainy texture may be pleasant to eat, but it cannot verify every benefit claimed on a package.
